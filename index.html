Plains game

Terrain runs from open plains to mountainous, dense bushveld, so each concession and each day hunts differently.

Kudu, sable and gemsbuck through to colour variants like king's wildebeest and black impala.

Bowhunting

Blinds and areas set up for compound, crossbow, recurve and longbow hunters. No permits are needed to bring a bow into South Africa.

Most shots fall between 10 and 40 yards. Book the dry months and close to the dark moon for your best odds.

Best months
July to September
Typical range
10 to 40 yards
Paperwork
None for bows

Wing shooting

Over 75,000 acres of crops, trained bird dogs with a handler, full decoy spreads and well equipped vehicles.

Doves, rock pigeon, Natal and Swainson's spurfowl, helmeted guineafowl, plus a variety of ducks and geese.

Photographic safaris

Kruger National Park

Nearly two million hectares and your best shot at seeing the Big Five up close. Dinner under the stars to the sound of lions and hyenas.

Cape Town

Wine farms, top restaurants and the view from Table Mountain, one of the New Seven Wonders of Nature.

Victoria Falls

Under two hours' flight from Johannesburg. Tiger fishing, houseboat cruises and bungee jumping off the bridge.

We suggest three to four days per destination. A personal guide stays with you from arrival to departure.

What to pack

  • Passport valid 6 months from entry, with two blank pages
  • Travelling with kids: unabridged birth certificate and consent from both parents
  • Earth-tone clothing, comfortable shoes, a hat and light jackets. Laundry is done daily.
  • Binoculars, rangefinder, camera, sunblock, bug spray and any medication

Trophies and taxidermy

Skins are dipped, salted and folded in our skinning shed. The taxidermist collects them after your hunt and handles the export paperwork to your home country. Have them mounted in South Africa, or ship dipped skins home to your own taxidermist.
